Two · App sheet, Logs

Find in the logs

Why: every log view shows a fixed last 200 lines with only Read again. Reading logs is what people do when something is broken, and today it means copying the block into an editor to search. A filter, an errors switch and more lines cover it; "Container" becomes "Logs from" with the app's own names.

Three · App sheet, Settings

Choices that say what they do

Why: a manifest's options are drawn as their raw values, so Pi-hole's listening mode offers "all, local, single, bind" under a 130-word hint, and its upstream DNS offers two IP addresses. A manifest option gains a label and a line of its own. The sheet also comes back as you left it when an approval is cancelled, which three sheets do by hand and two forget.
