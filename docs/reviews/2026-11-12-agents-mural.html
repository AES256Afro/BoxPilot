The server had room. The agents didn't.

Five read-only sweeps, a month of the server's own runs, and a timing of both models on its disk. The limits that stopped the agents were ours, not the machine's: a quarter of its memory in use, load under one. So the limits were raised, the loop that wasted them was fixed, and the rest is laid out below as six milestones.

How it learns the platform · M68 and M70

Seven methods, cheapest and most useful first.

Today the system prompt gives BoxPilot one sentence. The agent can search the docs, the operations and the catalog by keyword, and it knows no page names at all, although two templates are told to "name the page to open".

  1. A "where do I" toolS to M

    Returns the page, the feature, the operation and its tier in under 600 characters, from the index the command bar already builds.

  2. A platform card generated at releaseS

    One chunk per page and per area of operations, searched by meaning, never pasted into the prompt.

  3. Learn from a dismissed cardS to M

    Dismissals are stored and nothing reads them. The same proposal should not come back for a while.

  4. Seed examples written from the tool listS to M

    Three to five phrasings per tool, generated off the server, checked by the existing gate.

  5. Golden questions from the catalogM

    "Which port does it open", "what is it for": a rotating nightly sample.

  6. Release notes in the indexS to M

    So answers follow the version that is installed.

  7. An adapter trained off the serverL

    The recipe exists. Fix first: the test questions are in the training book, there is no held-out split, and one run a side is not a measurement.
